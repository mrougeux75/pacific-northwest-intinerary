    How to use this site

    Use the arrows to move day by day. Tap any map link to open navigation. The Tickets and Checklist tabs are stored in this browser, so edits and checked items remain on this device.

    Important

    Park access, permits, wildfire closures, tides, trail conditions, road construction, and operating hours can change. Verify each day shortly before departure.

    Editing the itinerary

    Open data.js in GitHub, click the pencil icon, change text or links, and commit the edit. GitHub Pages usually updates within a few minutes.

    Tickets & confirmations

    Tap a field to add your private confirmation details. They remain only in this browser.
